# Puffer Pond - Lexicon

> Generated from LLM Workbench v2.3.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-17
**Status:** active

## Ownership Rules

Definitions live here after they are settled. Requirements and decisions remain
in BLUEPRINT.md or the owning stable spec; active work remains in specs and the
generated Taskboard.

## Workbench Terms

| Term | Definition | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Design concept | The shared understanding of what Puffer Pond is and how it should feel. | BLUEPRINT.md and the concept image help participants reconstruct it; neither replaces the shared understanding. |
| Blueprint | Stable product direction, cross-cutting architecture, invariants, and non-goals. | Not a queue, glossary, PRD bundle, or proof archive. |
| Spec | Stable record for one cohesive capability, including requirements, decisions, tickets, acceptance, and evidence. | Its path remains stable after completion. |
| Ticket | Temporary vertical outcome inside one spec. | Not a second capability record or external tracker. |
| Taskboard | Generated projection of hot specs and current owner decisions. | Never the durable requirements or evidence owner. |

## Project Terms

| Term | Definition | Distinction / aliases to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Puffer Pond | The shipped full-screen ambient pond website and active Workbench visual-product test project. | Not a game, aquarium simulator, or generic benchmark fixture. |
| Ambient experience | The immediately watchable one-viewport shell, controls, local preference, sound, and ripple interactions. | Creature scheduling and visual asset fidelity have separate specs. |
| Pond family | Five distinct pea-puffer movement profiles plus visible snails below the waterline. | Does not imply breeding, needs, inventory, or progression. |
| Rare visitor | An intermittent hummingbird or dog visit scheduled independently from duck landings. | Ducks are surface visitors with their own cadence, not a rare-visitor kind in simulation.ts. |
| Waterline discipline | The responsive rule keeping aquatic life below water and shoreline/sky visitors at or above their intended layer. | Not one fixed pixel coordinate across viewports. |
| Real-phone watch | Kayden's five-minute experiential check of cropping, control comfort, smoothness, thermal/battery feel, calmness, and repetition. | It is an owner verdict, not an agent screenshot or synthetic performance claim. |
| Visual target | The approved gouache nature-book direction represented by docs/concept-desktop.png and the fidelity contract in S-004. | It does not require reusing the concept image as the production background. |
| PWA direction | The unresolved choice whether the link-based static site should also be installable and reload offline. | Feasibility does not make it agent-ready. |
